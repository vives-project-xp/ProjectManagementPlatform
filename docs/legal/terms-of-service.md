# Terms of Service: VIVES PMP

*Last updated: 19 November 2026*

**VIVES PMP** is the Project Management Platform built by a student team of VIVES University of Applied Sciences (Electronics-ICT, Project Experience, Brugge) for coaches and students of Project Experience. These terms cover its connection to social media accounts such as TikTok and Instagram.

## What the app does

- Students upload weekly progress updates (text, photos and short videos) for their Project. Before uploading, they confirm that everyone recognisable agrees.
- An AI suggests a social media post from an update. A Teacher reviews it, edits it if needed, and accepts or refuses it.
- An accepted post is published at a planned time on the **team's own** account, here the team's TikTok account, through the platform's official APIs.

The app only publishes to accounts that the team itself owns and has connected. It never publishes to, or reads from, anyone else's account.

## Who may use it

Only Users the platform's Superuser created: VIVES coaches and students of Project Experience, and Clients they invited. Social media accounts are connected only by the team that owns them.

## Content

The Students who made the photos, videos and texts, and the Projects they belong to, remain responsible for that content, under the rules of VIVES and of the social media platform concerned. A Teacher reviews every post before it is published.
