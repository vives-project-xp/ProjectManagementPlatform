# Privacy Policy: VIVES PMP

*Last updated: 19 November 2026*

**VIVES PMP** is the Project Management Platform built by a student team of VIVES University of Applied Sciences for Project Experience. This policy covers what the platform does with data from social media platforms such as TikTok and Instagram. For everything else about how VIVES handles personal data, the VIVES privacy statement applies: https://www.vives.be/nl/over-vives/privacyverklaring

## What we get from TikTok

The team connects **only its own** TikTok account. For that account, the platform keeps:

- the **access token** (and refresh token) TikTok issues when the team logs in, so that it can publish on the team's behalf;
- the account's basic profile (`user.info.basic`: id and display name), to show which account is connected;
- the ids and links of the posts it published, to show them to the team.

We don't collect or read data of other TikTok users: no followers, no comments, no messages and no viewing data.

## What we send to TikTok

Only the content of posts a Teacher approved: the chosen video or photos, and the caption. Before uploading, Students confirm that everyone recognisable in them agrees to being posted on VIVES social media.

## Where it is kept

The tokens are stored only on the platform's own server at VIVES and in the deployment's configuration. They are never shown in the app and never shared. Posts can be deleted on TikTok at any time by the team.

## How long

The tokens are kept while the account is connected and removed when the team disconnects it. Media that were never posted are deleted 4 months after their week.

## Your rights and contact

Students and staff can ask VIVES about their data through the VIVES privacy statement above. The student team can be reached through https://github.com/vives-project-xp/ProjectManagementPlatform
